1.2 Project Objective & Scope
Objective: Helvian will provide an operational web application for managing clinical departments, patient workflow records, clinical appointments, care coordination tasks, operational activities, and patient workflow reporting. The project includes a Laravel web application, backend and SQL database, separate product landing page, and QA before deployment.

1.3 Product Definition & Business Model
Product Type: Clinical Operations and Patient Workflow Management Web Application

1.4 Functional Application Architecture
Frontend & Backend: PHP / Laravel
Database: SQL
Authentication: Administrator, Clinical Manager, Doctor, Nurse and Care Coordinator roles
Data Management: Patient profiles, departments, appointments, workflow stages, care coordination tasks and operational records
Out of Scope: Clinical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, medical image interpretation, electronic health record integrations, real-time patient monitoring and direct NVIDIA MONAI deployment. The MVP focuses on administrative and workflow management using demonstration data.
